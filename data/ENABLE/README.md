# ENABLE word list

`enable1.txt` is the ENABLE word list (Enhanced North American Benchmark
LExicon): 172,820 lowercase English words, one per line, with every inflected
form listed and no proper nouns or abbreviations. The `mishear` crate uses it
to keep names, brands and junk out of its vocabulary, as rhyme-golf does.

Source: <https://norvig.com/ngrams/enable1.txt> (SHA-256 `61ba1392a5b6199d…`).

ENABLE is in the public domain: its master list was "formally released into
the Public Domain" (see the ENABLE README packaged with SCOWL, e.g.
<https://sources.debian.org/src/scowl/6-2/r/enable/README>).

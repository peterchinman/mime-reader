form: triolet
title: Triolet (Easy is the Triolet)
author: W. E. Henley
year: 1887
source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45736
notes: text from Gleeson White, ed., Ballades and Rondeaus, Chants Royal, Sestinas, Villanelles, &c. (1887), Gutenberg #45736

Easy is the Triolet,
If you really learn to make it!
Once a neat refrain you get,
Easy is the Triolet.
As you see!-I pay my debt
With another rhyme. Deuce take it,
Easy is the Triolet,
If you really learn to make it!

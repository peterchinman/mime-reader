form: triolet
title: "Urceus Exit"
author: Austin Dobson
year: 1887
source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45736
notes: text from Gleeson White, ed., Ballades and Rondeaus, Chants Royal, Sestinas, Villanelles, &c. (1887), Gutenberg #45736; from the triolet group Rose-Leaves; italics removed

I intended an Ode,
And it turned to a Sonnet,
It began à la mode,
I intended an Ode;
But Rose crossed the road
In her latest new bonnet.
I intended an Ode,
And it turned to a Sonnet.

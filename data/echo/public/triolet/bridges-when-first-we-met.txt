form: triolet
title: Triolet (When first we met we did not guess)
author: Robert Bridges
year: 1912
source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/37804
notes: text from The Poetical Works of Robert Bridges (Oxford, 1912), Gutenberg #37804

When first we met we did not guess
That Love would prove so hard a master;
Of more than common friendliness
When first we met we did not guess.
Who could foretell this sore distress,
This irretrievable disaster
When first we met?--We did not guess
That Love would prove so hard a master.

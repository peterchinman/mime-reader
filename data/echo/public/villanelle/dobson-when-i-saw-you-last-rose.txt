form: villanelle
title: When I saw you last, Rose
author: Austin Dobson
year: 1887
source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45736
notes: text from Gleeson White, ed., Ballades and Rondeaus, Chants Royal, Sestinas, Villanelles, &c. (1887), Gutenberg #45736; dashes are single hyphens in this transcription

When I saw you last, Rose,
You were only so high;-
How fast the time goes!

Like a bud ere it blows,
You just peeped at the sky,
When I saw you last, Rose!

Now your petals unclose,
Now your May-time is nigh;-
How fast the time goes!

And a life,-how it grows!
You were scarcely so shy,
When I saw you last, Rose!

In your bosom it shows
There's a guest on the sly;
How fast the time goes!

Is it Cupid? Who knows!
Yet you used not to sigh,
When I saw you last, Rose;-
How fast the time goes!
